Reading the pattern

You win capabilities. You lose situations.

Pattern

Every question you swept 3 of 3 is phrased as “who does X”. Every question you lost 0 of 3 is phrased as “we have this problem” or “should we do A or B”. Buyers ask the second kind first — they describe their situation before they know what to call the service.

Fix Write the situation-shaped pages: the trade contractor with a dead website, the business stuck on page 3, the non-profit, the map pack.
Hurts

Web design is your headline service and you are invisible on it. Asked for Edmonton web design companies, you were named in 0 of 3 runs. Top Draw, Pixel Army, Box Clever and Web3 were named instead — and we can see the model searching for them by name while it answers.

Fix A real web design service page. Today /services/web-design/ redirects to a competitor-comparison landing page.
Encouraging

Virtual tours you sweep 3 of 3 — and it is not declared anywhere in your llms.txt. Something other than the file you wrote for AI is already working in your favour there, which means these wins are earnable.

Critical

Five of the eleven links in your llms.txt are dead. It is the one file on your site written specifically so AI models can find and describe you. Your case studies, your About page and three of your services return nothing to a model that follows it. Two more land on the wrong page.

Six services that do exist — virtual tours, web hosting, video marketing, graphic design, print and marketing strategy — are not declared at all. The file also reads as keyword copy (“a go-to Edmonton SEO company”) rather than the plain factual description these files are meant to carry.

Fix Rebuild it against the live sitemap, in hours, not days. Is this what costs you the lost questions? Not on its own — you win virtual tours without declaring it. But right now the single asset you built for AI points it at five empty pages.

Method. Unbranded prompts are questions a buyer would type without knowing BubbleUP exists; only those count toward the headline figure. A mention counts when the brand or an alias appears in the answer text. A citation counts when bubbleup.ca appears among the sources the model opened. Confidence intervals are Wilson 95%.

Limits. One engine (Gemini with Google Search), 78 answers, one market, one day. Enough to establish position and direction with confidence intervals; not enough to claim a trend over time, and it does not tell you how ChatGPT, Claude or Perplexity answer the same questions — they retrieve differently and the numbers will differ.
